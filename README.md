# Peixi Xiong Projects Index

🏠 [Peixi Xiong Homepage](https://pixel-xiong.github.io/)

Lightweight directory of selected applied research projects. Some repositories are experimental or confidential. Items outside Intel are labeled explicitly.

---

## Textual Visual Logic Challenge (Intel, ECCV 2024)
🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/IntelLabs/Textual-Visual-Logic-Challenge)  
📄 [Paper](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/papers/00796.pdf)

A benchmark and dataset introduced at ECCV 2024 for evaluating reasoning intensive text to image generation, emphasizing compositional relations, structured scene logic, and systematic evaluation of visual reasoning ability.

---

## OpenVINO Multi Granularity Profiling (Intel, Confidential)
🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/intel-sandbox/openvino-multigranularity-profiler)

An internal experimental toolkit for profiling AI workloads across heterogeneous devices using the OpenVINO runtime, designed to analyze performance behavior and system level bottlenecks.

---

## Multi Agent Deep Research for Document QA (Intel, Confidential)
🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/intel-sandbox/multi-agent-deep-research-officeqa-langgraph)

An internal research prototype exploring agent based pipelines for document grounded question answering and structured information extraction.

---

## Internal Agentic Hybrid AI Workbench (Intel, Confidential)
🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/intel-sandbox/multi-agent-workbench-eval-gdpval)

Internal workbench for running agentic, hybrid AI workflows grounded in proprietary documents, with structured task execution and traceable outputs.

---

## Structured Alignment VQA (Microsoft Research Internship)
🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/Peixixiong/Structured-Alignment-VQA)  
📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.10654)

A Visual Question Answering approach that improves cross modal reasoning through structured alignment between visual regions and semantic representations, developed during a Microsoft Research internship.
